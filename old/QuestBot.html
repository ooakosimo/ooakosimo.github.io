The Mission: Gamify the friction of daily life. Track quests, calculate loadouts, and monitor resource burn rates in real-time.

1. Phase I: Planning Room Mode

While preparing to leave. Add tasks to calculate required cash-on-hand.

/q [Task] [MaxPrice] Ex: /q Cat Food 120 Ex: /q Fix Appearance 0 (Ritual)

Check Loadout:

/check Bot returns: "🎒 Cash Needed: ₱920"

2. Phase II: Adventure Road Mode

Execute tasks. If the Actual Price is lower than your Estimate, the bot logs it as Savings.

/done [Task] [ActualPrice] Ex: /done Cat Food 105 Result: "✅ Complete! 🎉 Saved ₱15!"

Impulse Buys (Side Quests):
Did you buy snacks you didn't plan for?

/side [Item] [Cost] Ex: /side Turon 20

Looting (PSG):
Did you get something for free (e.g., from parents)?

/loot [Item] [Value] Ex: /loot Sugar 80 Result: "💎 Loot Secured! Saved ₱80!"

3. Phase III: Debrief Stats

Where did the money go? Review your performance.

/burned
