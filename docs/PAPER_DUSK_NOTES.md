# PAPER DUSK :: gentle panic, slow dance

**live at:** [ooakosimo.neocities.org/dusk](https://ooakosimo.neocities.org/dusk/)

if you have time, just try enjoy the piece first. it's alive :.

**Move. Stay. Leave it alone.**

interactive conceptual piece meant to be watched full screen. a whole cycle
is 4 minutes. i made it for leaving on my external monitor to relax my eyes
while listening to music — designed to help induce the mind to wander, like
a massage for your mindspace haha

## LINEAGE ::

this traces back to Craig Reynolds' **boids** (1986). he built it trying to
capture the thing i used to sit and watch for hours in busy streets and train
station crowds: no conductor, no central plan, just thousands of agents each
following their own small rules — keep distance, match your neighbors, drift
toward the group — and out of it comes this coherent flow that looks
choreographed.

traffic on EDSA is a flocking system. rush hour at the MRT is a flocking
system. crowds crossing at Cubao are a flocking system. watching emergence
with the naked eye. being in it and apart from it at the same time.

## THE PIECE ::

three species of paper — **cranes, planes, torn scraps** — share one flock.
untouched, they follow an invisible current. every so often something unseen
spooks them: a ripple, a scatter, a regroup. nobody dies, nobody wins, life
continues.

the sky crossfades through **four dusks** too slowly to catch in the act —
deliberate choices so the sky always heals. no scars, no memory. the only
memory of the panic lives in you. the glitched CRT scanlines are an accident
i kept.

## TECH ::

p5.js, one self-contained html file, no build step, hosted on neocities.
phone / tablet / desktop friendly.
