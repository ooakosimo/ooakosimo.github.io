# QUICKSTART :: OOAK INSTRUMENTS

*One hand-coded webpage is the artwork. Your phone is the remote. That's the whole machine.*

## VISITORS — three steps
1. **Scan the QR** next to the projection → the CONTROL ROOM opens. Nothing installs; no permissions are asked.
2. **Invent a pilot name**, type the room word from the placard, press **TAKE THE CONTROLS**.
3. **Fly.** Sliders change the artwork in real time. Press **⚡ FLASH MY NAME** to put your pilot name on the wall for a moment. Be legendary, be kind.

Several pilots at once? Yes — last touch wins, and the projection shows who's flying. That's the game.

## ON A COMPUTER
Open the piece's page in any browser. Keys:

| key | does |
|---|---|
| **S** | settings panel (all sliders + colors) |
| **P** | pin mode — drag the four corners to map onto anything |
| **F** | fullscreen |
| **M** | microphone reactivity on/off (asks permission first) |
| **0** | hide/show the HUD |

Drag any photo onto the ChonkParade page to feed the melt.

## CURATORS / INSTALLERS — five steps
1. Power the projector, connect its WiFi (once).
2. Open the launcher URL in its browser; pick the piece with the remote.
3. Aim, lock the projector down, then map: press **P** (USB mouse) and drag corners onto the surface — **or** unlock the *curator deck* in the CONTROL ROOM (ask the artist for the word) and nudge corners from your phone with the trackpad.
4. Corners save on the device and survive reboots. **Reset mapping** lives in the panel and the curator deck.
5. Do **not** enable auto-launch until the piece has run stable for a day. If anything ever goes truly sideways: the projector's Settings → Factory Reset restores it completely — nothing we install touches firmware.
