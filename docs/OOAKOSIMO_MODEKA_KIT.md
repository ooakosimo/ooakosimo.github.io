## 1 · THE OPENING PARAGRAPH

**OOAKOSIMO builds living systems that make invisible forces visible.** Rather
than creating images of bureaucracy, surveillance, perception, or memory, she
constructs systems that behave. Each work is unfinished by design, responding
to sound, interaction, and time so that no two encounters are alike. Her
practice moves between live generative performance and interactive conceptual
works, investigating how invisible structures — social, technological, and
psychological — shape human experience, while inviting the viewer to become a
participant in the work's unfolding.

## 3 · THE THREE PIECES (this show)

**CHONK PARADE :: melt procession**
A procession of hand-drawn walkers crossing a slow photographic melt — decay
as a medium, attention as a signal. Visitors steer the procession from their
own phones and can flash their names across the wall: present for a moment,
then surrendered to the melt, like everything else. The creatures descend from
the artist's own shadow practice (2017; first published as *shadow realm*,
2020), with a credited nod to Marko Zubak, a.k.a. Mlibty, who blessed the
derivations by name.

**LIFE AS WE KEEP ON KEEPING ON**
A playable essay about commuter trauma and algorithmic crowds: a voxel world
of small runners crossing terrain that shifts between serenity and static.
Visitors hold the switch between order and collapse — the daily commute's real
question — and the crowd keeps moving either way. On their own phones: drag to
orbit, pinch to zoom.

**PAPER DUSK :: gentle panic, slow dance**
An origami flock in four dusk palettes — cranes, planes, and torn scraps
sharing one sky. Untouched, they follow an invisible current; every so often
something unseen spooks them — a ripple, a scatter, a regroup. Nobody dies,
nobody wins, life continues. The sky completes a full dusk cycle every
~4 minutes, crossfading too slowly to catch in the act, so it always heals —
no scars, no memory. The only memory of the panic lives in you. One line of
lineage: descends from Craig Reynolds' *boids* (1986) — flocking born from
watching real crowds move with no conductor. **Why this wall:** the longest
queue in the house deserves the calmest work in it — a 4-minute sky means
everyone in line sees at least one whole dusk. On the TV: move the wired
mouse to gather, hold to scatter. On phones via the wall QR: same flock,
pocket edition.

**HOW VISITORS FLY IT — the four steps**
1. Scan the QR (or type **ooakosimo.neocities.org/console**). Nothing installs.
2. Pick the piece in the dropdown — *chonk parade* or *commute deck*.
3. Invent a pilot name → **TAKE THE CONTROLS**.
4. Sliders move the light in real time. **⚡ FLASH MY NAME** puts your name on
   the projection — it lingers, breathes, and melts away.

*(PAPER DUSK plays differently: no console — the wired mouse at the TV, or
scan its wall QR and the flock lives on your own phone.)*

## 4 · TALKING POINTS (for the floor)

- Everything is a **single hand-coded HTML file** — no app, no engine, no
  template. View-source is welcome; the code is the artwork.
- Nothing installs on visitors' phones; the browser page IS the remote.
  **One QR flies both console pieces.**
- It's **running live, not a video** — never the same twice, and it's
  listening.
- **PAPER DUSK's sky completes a full dusk cycle every ~4 minutes** — anyone
  in the queue sees at least one whole dusk.
- The artist spent **20+ years in film & broadcast post-production** (her IMDb
  credits include *Iskul Bukol the Movie*) before abandoning the fixed
  timeline entirely.
- She has been **minting on-chain since February 1, 2020**, and has taught
  hundreds of artists to mint their own work.
- She was **raised inside Manila's 1980s underground**: she watched
  sound-sculptor **Lirio Salvador** build his *Sandata* instruments from
  scrap; punk figure **Bobby Balingit of The Wuds** is now her neighbor — she
  animates his paintings. The lineage is ongoing, not historical.
- The creatures' shadow lineage is **credited and blessed by name**
  (Marko Zubak, a.k.a. Mlibty).
- License, if asked: **individuals remix freely with credit; commercial use

**ooakosimo.neocities.org/commute/** (it may already be set) → press **F** for

browser to the same address.
