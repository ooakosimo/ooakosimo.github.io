# Alamat ng Pinya: The Thousand-Eyed Satellite

**OOAKOSIMO** · HTML / JavaScript / p5.js (WebGL) · Edition 1 of 1 · Collection: Field Notes from the Balcony · Part of the Buko Piña Pie series

A 3D generative reimagining of the Filipino folk tale. The traditional Alamat ng Pinya is about the transition from childhood laziness to a state of "Total Awareness." In the story, a daughter named Pina is transformed into a fruit with a thousand eyes, the Pinya, becoming a silent, omniscient observer.

In this Web3 reimagining, the Pinya is no longer a symbol of punishment but of Neurological Sovereignty. The "Thousand Eyes" represent the high-fidelity awareness required to navigate the glitch. The organic spiky body is fused with mechanical tubes and industrial smoke, suggesting a "pumping" life-support system for the creative mind.

This piece was coded while in a state of physical recovery, capturing the moment where the chaos of the world is observed, processed, and refined into bit-crushed gold.

## Interactive protocol

- **Mouse / touch drag**: rotate the camera to observe the Pinya from any angle.
- **Mouse / touch position**: controls the "Chaos Variable." Faster movement triggers spastic glitch extrusions and intensified industrial pumping.
